How It Works

Clear scope. Clear schedule. No disappearing contractor act.

We keep smaller home projects organized with a simple three-step process so you know what is happening, what it costs, and what comes next.

  1. 1. Tell us what needs attention. Send photos and a short description of the project.
  2. 2. Confirm the plan. We clarify scope, timing, and expected cost before work begins.
  3. 3. Get it off the list. Our team completes the agreed work and confirms the final result with you.
